## 1. Sharp visibility equivalence (finite laws)
Let X be a finite-valued vector of realized repair events in a fixed room/claim and a predeclared concurrent portfolio. X_j=0 if route j does not actually produce a verified correction by the horizon; a route that is not attempted is not a missing success. A public log reveals all X when R=1 and only a missing symbol when R=0. Under hypothesis H let p_H(x) be the actual distribution; policy-dependent visibility e_H(x)=P_H(R=1|X=x) may depend on X. Suppose common bounds l<=e_H(x)<=u and prescribed common log rate rho. The public log law can be identical across all H iff

    L_x=l max_H p_H(x), U_x=u min_H p_H(x),
    L_x<=U_x for all x, and sum_x L_x<=rho<=sum_x U_x.

Proof: common visible subprobability q(x)=p_H(x)e_H(x) must lie in the intersection of intervals for every H and sum to rho. Necessity follows directly. Sufficiency follows by filling the box [L,U] continuously to the desired total and defining e_H=q/p_H when p_H>0 (arbitrary allowed e_H otherwise). The unlogged mass is 1-rho in all worlds. This criterion is a simple finite-law selection identity, not a new general missing-data theorem.

For the V2 parity pair p_0=2^-m, p_S=p_0(1+epsilon chi_S), chi_S=(-1)^(sum_{i in S}x_i), and 0<l<=u, overlap of the two per-state intervals requires epsilon<=1-l/u; common coverage must also be in [l(1+epsilon/2),u(1-epsilon/2)]. A particularly useful simultaneous construction for all menu alternatives is q=rho*p_0, with 0<rho<1-epsilon and unrestricted visibility in [0,1]. It is feasible because e_0=rho and e_S=rho/(1+epsilon chi_S)<=1. Even high coverage can be completely uninformative: epsilon=.2, rho=.79 (not exactly .8 for strict positive residual support). All observed records and the missing rate are identical, but the true failure of S changes from 2^-r to (1+epsilon)2^-r. These examples are constructed, not observed AI behavior.

## 2. Adaptive policy blindness
Take two policies A,B and two worlds: A has p0 and B has pS in world 0; swap in world 1. Their public logs have the common law above at each fresh scenario; initial answer accuracy is fixed equal by design. Any learner choosing policies based only on past public records has the same joint transcript distribution in both worlds (induct on time; conditional action kernel and public observation law are shared). If the target is to minimize failure of designated S, the expected one-step regret in at least one world is >= epsilon*2^-r/2; over T rounds worst-case expected cumulative regret >= T*epsilon*2^-r/2. Proof: expected number of A choices n_A is identical across worlds, losses are g(T-n_A) and gn_A, g=epsilon*2^-r. Average is Tg/2. This is a restricted indistinguishability construction, not a theorem for arbitrary evolving networks. It illustrates how a policy can change actual outcomes while its own logs conceal the change. It does not imply malicious intent.

## 3. Exact post-outcome audit frontier
In the common q construction, the conditional residual law for unlogged scenes is

    P0(X|R=0)=p0;
    PS(X|R=0)=p0[1+epsilon/(1-rho)*chi_S].

Thus the hidden perturbation is concentrated in missing scenes, not erased independently as in V2. Let a=1-rho, e'=epsilon/a in (0,1), d'=-log(1-e'^2)/2, h'=kl(2^-r,(1+e')2^-r). Each fresh encounter costs b>0, whether logged or missing. After seeing R, an independent auditor can read a vector of s realized outcomes at cost c_s or one direct portfolio failure bit at cost c_D. Readout occurs after the outcome, does not alter it, and is only useful for R=0; it is lawful, technically available, and complete when purchased. Every encounter is fresh iid. No unrecorded human action is magically reconstructed. The rate for safe-world menu certification is

    eta_post=max{ max_s>=r d' binom(s,r)/(M*(c_s+b/a)),
                  h'/(M*(c_D+b/a)) }, M=binom(m,r).

Any sequential test certifying H0 with probability >=1-alpha, and falsely certifying any H_S with probability <=beta, alpha+beta<1, has E0[cost]>=kl(1-alpha,beta)/eta_post. For fixed finite family/costs and symmetric error delta ->0, first-order safe-world optimum equals log(1/delta)/eta_post.

Proof. Public log KL is zero. Conditional residual channel KL equals d' for S subset A, h' for the corresponding direct bit, zero otherwise (V2 parity marginalization). The chain rule and data processing give M kl(1-alpha,beta) <= sum of expected per-action KL summed over alternatives. Let N be number of started encounters; since R is iid with missing probability a and starting each encounter is predictable, E0[N_missing]=a E0[N] (finite expectation). If K paid missing readouts occur, E0[K]<=a E0[N]. Hence E0[cost]>=sum_j E0[K_j](c_j+b/a), ignoring useless readouts of already logged scenes. Sum-of-KL bound and symmetry give lower bound. For achievability, choose best action type and uniformly choose the portfolio/subset on every missing scene; no readouts on logged scenes. Conditional log likelihood increments per encounter are iid bounded, with per-alternative positive mean a times the uniform mean KL; expected cost per encounter is b+a*c. Reject if any log likelihood L_S<=-log(M/delta); certify if all L_S>=log(1/delta). Likelihood-ratio martingales provide error control; bounded-increment concentration and the V2 tail argument give expected safe-world stopping time asymptotically at threshold divided by mean information. Wald's identity applies to iid per-encounter costs and predictable starts. This gives first-order optimality under the stated family, not a general optimality claim for informative visibility. For rho>=1-epsilon the strict full-support construction no longer applies. Other common q laws need separate KL calculations and cost-normalized design; general controlled sensing covers that problem.

## 4. Creation is not censoring
Define C(a,z) = probability of verified collective correction by a predeclared horizon under AI policy a and a randomized prospective access intervention z (e.g. distinct-source verification budget). Let natural correction C(a,0) be the primary endpoint. A retrospective readout of actually realized outcomes identifies missing natural outcomes under successful random follow-up, not C(a,1). Two worlds can have identical fully observed natural paths under z=0 but all previously failed cases respond to z=1 in one world and none in the other. Without assumptions C(a,1) lies in [0,1], or [C(a,0),1] under monotonicity of repair (not assumed in the main experiment). This is standard counterfactual non-identification, not a novel theorem. It rules out extrapolating from more logs to potential evidence that was never acquired. Use prospective randomization of access and retrospective randomization of readout separately; estimate policy-by-access interaction as a difference-in-differences across randomized arms, not as mediation without extra assumptions. If audit anticipation affects behavior, define separate consented monitoring regimes and do not claim a nonreactive estimator.

## 5. Robust certification rather than structural self-confirmation
With actual passive portfolio outcomes and random representative follow-up of missing scenes, write failure f_T=rho*p_{T,logged}+(1-rho)*p_{T,missing}. For independent fixed-size samples, simultaneous exact binomial confidence intervals for the stratum probabilities and coverage yield a conservative confidence set by union bound. Extremize the affine mixture over the confidence rectangle (vertices suffice); certify only if every portfolio upper bound is below a fixed threshold. Treat unknown or inaccessible missing outcomes as a partial-identification interval, not as zero failures. This approach is standard stratified inference. In human trials use cluster-level randomization and design-appropriate inference instead of independent Bernoulli assumptions on participants in one room. Structural model/LLM/graph estimates may prioritize queries, never replace held-out direct randomized portfolio certification. All claims about OR require noninterfering concurrently realized paths; if activating routes changes others, directly randomize the portfolio and measure the actual room endpoint.

## 6. Reproducible computational checks
Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python audit_v3.py` (numpy, scipy). 160 feasibility LP comparisons, 504 frontier LPs, and 5,364 conditional KL identities passed. The 160 random feasibility tests alone are not balanced evidence; constructive parity examples and analytic proof provide separate positive checks. The code generated 120,000 sequential synthetic trials and 36,000 fixed-audit robust trials. Conditions: m=5,r=3,epsilon=.4,rho=.5; all public logs are identical; target hidden failure .175 versus safe .125. For the structural stress case a pairwise parity interaction replaces the assumed triple; three-way parity marginals still look benign but some true triple failure probabilities are .175. Cost units are abstract, encounter b=.2, full vector cost 5; direct cost .15 or 1. Sequential trials use fixed thresholds and hard budget reservation, 2,000 replicates per cell, inconclusive outcomes retained. Vector-all pays for readout on every case including logged cases; this is intentionally a wasteful baseline, not a state-of-the-art method. Direct-gold observes the unconditional portfolio failure and ignores public logs; direct-missing uses missing-stratum likelihood. Public-only cannot validly distinguish and makes no decision. All structural tests use oracle known family and parameters, not estimated from real data.

At cost budget 300 and direct cost 1, safe certification: vector-all 83.90%, vector-missing 96.45%; average spend 192.65 vs 103.73; unsafe rejection: 95.15% vs 99.55%. False certification under specified unsafe alternative 0.60% vs 0.35%. Under the out-of-family pair alternative vector-missing falsely certifies 96.50%. Direct strategies remain mostly inconclusive at this budget. Do not represent this as general superiority over direct audits. The structural methods are not distribution-free.

Robust check uses fixed numbers of representative missing-stratum audits, known public conditional law and known rho (oracle simplification), Bonferroni Clopper–Pearson intervals, and maximum allowable failure .15. The budget is expected, not a hard cap, due to waiting for missing scenes. At expected budget 10,000 and direct cost 1, robust vector certifies 99.85% of safe worlds versus 21.25% direct (equal expected costs, 1851 vs 714 observations per portfolio), rejects 99.65% of in-family unsafe worlds vs 76.00%, and rejects 100% of off-model unsafe worlds vs 98.50%. Zero observed false certifications in both unsafe worlds for both robust strategies (2,000 replicates each). No claim that error is literally zero or that robust method at budget 10,000 is directly comparable to structural method at budget 300. Unknown public-law estimation and misspecified follow-up access remain tests for later work. Real budget, clustering, longitudinal dynamics and actual LLM/human studies remain unvalidated.

## 7. Research programme: falsifiable human–AI core
**Main question:** Can AI interfaces induce a loss of independent correction pathways while simultaneously making ordinary records falsely reassuring, even when initial claim accuracy and available external evidence are held constant?

Use a consented, closed fictional-information environment. Randomize AI cue policy (e.g. source provenance and apparent corroboration displayed or withheld without fabricating real evidence) and independent verification access at room level. Fix initial claim truth, AI output text/accuracy and external evidence inventory; vary the cue or access mechanism independently. Randomize retrospective readout of existing consented traces after the endpoint, not secret surveillance; if unavailable use direct recorded outcome and report missingness. Verify claim truth by external adjudication; define correction by horizon and audience threshold, with true-claim false-correction guardrail. Track actual retrieval, independent evidence acquisition, delivery, belief uptake, effort and inequity. The primary estimand is the policy difference in natural correction under fixed access; the policy-by-access interaction measures whether restoring an independently sourced path changes correction differently by policy. Secondary: joint failure of concurrently realized paths and logged-vs-direct discrepancy; avoid post-treatment conditioning as a causal estimate. Cluster-aware pilot and power analysis before a confirmatory experiment. Compare no AI, provenance-only, verification affordance (JCMC 2026), independent aggregation, ordinary RAG, direct randomized audits, nonparametric double sampling, and calibrated robust methods at common cost.

Architecture: (1) frozen contemporary open-weight LLM with traceable retrieval, (2) temporal provenance/repair hypergraph used only for hypotheses, (3) prospective randomized access and retrospective readout, (4) robust endpoint certificate, (5) constrained policy updates with held-out fresh rooms and explicit uncertainty. Do not claim to have implemented hypergraph transformers or RL. If structure does not improve actual cost/power under fair comparison, use direct audit. If policy does not alter repair or observability in experiments, revise the mechanism. If no lawful passive readout exists, do not claim the retrospective frontier applies. The dynamic creation and visibility model with endogenous carryover remains an open theoretical target; current sequential proof concerns fresh scenarios, not a general performative RL solution.
