## Research target, scope and falsification
Let a room/claim be a controlled scenario with an independently adjudicated false claim, fixed starting evidence and assigned AI policy. A repair portfolio is a predeclared collection of concurrently executable verification channels. The main social outcome is correction of false belief after evidence discovery, delivery and uptake, at a predefined horizon and audience criterion. True claims provide a false-correction guardrail. Randomize AI cue policy and independent evidence access/delivery in independent rooms; measure actual checking, discovery, communication and belief. Do not equate intention to check with checking. Use matched initial accuracy and hold text/provenance constant where possible. If routing changes behavior, direct randomized portfolio outcomes are required; do not reconstruct counterfactual joint outcomes from distinct human participants. The proposed theory below concerns passive observation of a fixed joint law, not a proof about policy-induced interference.

## Theorem: symmetric repair-menu audit frontier (restricted model)
Consider m binary lane outcomes X on a fresh scenario. For 2 <= r <= m, a deployment may later choose any r-lane portfolio T. Let M = binom(m,r), and choose known 0 < e < 1. Null law P0 is uniform on {0,1}^m. Alternative P_S, for an unknown r-subset S, has mass 2^(-m)[1+e(-1)^(sum_{i in S} x_i)]. All laws have full support. Define failure of T as all zero on T. Under P0 all menu failure probabilities are p=2^(-r). Under P_S only portfolio S has failure p(1+e); every other r-portfolio has failure p. Certification is H0 versus any H_S, equivalent to all menu failure rates <= a threshold strictly between p and p(1+e) in this family only.

An action is chosen before observing a fresh iid scenario, possibly based on past scenarios. It either logs vector X_A for a chosen subset A of size s at cost c_s>0, or directly records the failure bit for an r-portfolio T at cost c_D>0. Logging does not change outcomes; no additional observations are available. Let d=-0.5 log(1-e^2) and h=kl(p,p(1+e)), with kl the binary relative entropy. Define

    eta = max { max_{r<=s<=m} [d binom(s,r)/(M c_s)], h/(M c_D) }.

For any sequential certifier with P0(certify)>=1-alpha, and P_S(certify)<=beta for every S, alpha+beta<1, finite expected cost under P0, the expected cost obeys

    E0[cost] >= kl(1-alpha,beta)/eta.

Furthermore for fixed finite m,r,e,c and alpha=beta=delta tending to zero, the infimum of E0[cost]/log(1/delta) tends to 1/eta. This is safe-world first-order optimality, not a minimax claim across alternative-world costs.

### Proof
1. Marginalizing the parity perturbation over a missing S-coordinate cancels it. Hence KL(P0^A || P_S^A)=d if S subset A and 0 otherwise. If S subset A, parity is equally likely under P0, yielding d by averaging -log(1+e) and -log(1-e). Direct failure bit has KL h when T=S, zero otherwise. The portfolio probabilities follow from the same marginalization.
2. Conditional on the past, an adaptive auditor's randomized action rule is shared by all hypotheses. The stopped-transcript KL chain rule gives the sum of expected action counts under P0 times the per-action KL. Finite expected cost and finite action alphabet with positive costs ensure finite expected sample count and bounded log-likelihood increments. By data processing to the certify indicator and monotonicity of binary KL (1-alpha>beta), every alternative imposes cumulative expected KL >= k=kl(1-alpha,beta).
3. Sum these M inequalities. A vector action of size s contributes d binom(s,r) to the sum; a direct action contributes h. Therefore the sum is <= M eta times the expected cost, giving the lower bound. Equivalently symmetrize any cost-normalized design under all coordinate permutations. Uniformly choosing a size-s action, or a direct portfolio, achieves equal information rates for all S and the largest eta is attained by the best single action type.
4. Achievability: use the best action type and sample uniformly within it on each independent scenario. Each log likelihood L_S(n)=log(P0(transcript)/P_S(transcript)) has bounded iid increments under P0, positive mean mu=c*eta, c the fixed per-step cost. Reject H0 if any L_S <= -log(M/delta); certify if all L_S >= log(1/delta). Under P0 exp(-L_S) is a nonnegative likelihood-ratio martingale, so Ville and union bound give false rejection <=delta; under P_S exp(L_S) is a likelihood-ratio martingale giving false certification <=delta. Under each alternative its own L_S has negative drift, so termination is almost sure. Under P0, let tau+ be first simultaneous crossing of the upper thresholds; stopping time <=tau+. For any fixed z>0 and n >= (1+z)log(1/delta)/mu, bounded-increment Hoeffding plus union bound gives P0(tau+>n)<=M exp(-k_z n) for some k_z>0, since tau+>n implies at least one L_S(n) below threshold. Summing this tail and letting z decrease to zero yields limsup E0[cost]/log(1/delta)<=1/eta. Binary KL lower bound yields matching liminf. QED.

### Corollaries and interpretation
- If only vector actions with s<r are available, eta=0; the hypotheses are observationally identical, and a valid finite certifier is impossible. Direct audits of every portfolio can still identify it, if available.
- If c_s=s (linear logging cost), binom(s,r)/s=binom(s-1,r-1)/r increases with s. Full-vector logging maximizes vector information per cost. Adaptive selection cannot beat the leading safe-world information rate in this symmetric family. If costs grow steeply, a smaller vector or a direct readout can win.
- For independent logging erasures with known common retention rho, observed mask independent of X and hypothesis, vector KL becomes d*rho^r when S subset A (by conditioning on mask), zero otherwise. Replace d with d*rho^r. This is exponential attenuation in interaction order, not a claim for endogenous missingness. If the policy changes what is logged based on X, the mask may itself convey information or hide it; the formula no longer follows.
- Distinguish menu certification from measuring a single deployed portfolio: a direct audit of that portfolio is an essential comparator and may dominate. A direct audit of an all-m OR is not assumed to be a menu audit; it has a different endpoint.
- This is a closed-form specialization of standard change-of-measure and controlled sensing, not a claim of a novel general theorem. The exact mixed vector/direct formula was not found verbatim in targeted search, but that is not proof of mathematical priority. The symmetry, parity family, independent scenarios and non-reactive logging are restrictive. No theorem about unrestricted human networks or RL follows.

## Next gates and architecture

For real human rooms, compare direct randomized portfolio audit, robust influence/verification baselines, general controlled sensing, and any structured method using identical cost accounting and direct held-out confirmation.
