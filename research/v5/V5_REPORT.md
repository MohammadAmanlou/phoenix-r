Search-indexed abstracts/excerpts inspected, not exhaustive full-text audit. HiddenBench/TIDE remain required external baselines for subsequent actual LLM experiments. The novelty candidate is a policy-induced change in collective repair dependencies AND visibility under a controlled intervention, not generic adaptive search, a Bayes DP or an e-process.

## Experiment 1: adaptive allocation
Each room has m persistent latent root labels. With probability 1/2 the claim is true and all roots invalid; otherwise each root is independently valid with probability p (possibly no valid roots). Each of B paid fresh measurements returns Bernoulli(s) for a valid root and Bernoulli(f) otherwise in the matched model. All policies share per-room truth and measurement tapes, and must use exactly B measurements. One root may be reported at the end; abstention utility 0, correct root +1, invalid root -1. Root LR >= m/alpha AND positive posterior expected utility is required. No correctness metadata visible to policy. Data generation, gate and prior explicit in adaptive_search.mjs. True-claim and false-claim groups equal in size. Model misspecification includes a uniform higher false positive rate and persistent root-specific hot nulls. Distinct from V4 dynamics; no relay/uptake modeled here.
Baselines: one root, cyclic, iid, SPRT-style heuristic (not full optimized replication of Malloy et al.), posterior greedy; PHOENIX lookahead is ONE-step expected terminal utility with tie-breaking by consecutive positive hits needed to cross threshold (not a trained model or general multi-step lookahead). Exact finite-horizon Bayesian DP only for m=3, B=8. DP recurrence V_t(h)=max_j E[V_(t-1)(h+observation_j)|h]; V_0 is max of zero and 2*q_j-1 over certified roots. Symmetry of exchangeable roots permits sorted count-pair memoization; posterior integrates the all-null/alternative mixture. 348 cached states, initial exact utility .15775472363538096; Monte Carlo utility .15266666666666667. Numerical agreement is a sanity check, not independent proof of optimality beyond stipulated model. Budget assertion each room.
186,000 policy-room trajectories, 31 policy/config rows, 3,000 per truth per row. Matched large: recovery rotate .40133, SPRT .613, greedy .625, lookahead .62933. Utility greedy .312 beats lookahead .301. Noisy large: greedy .42067 recovery, lookahead .43933; utilities .20883 and .211. No direct paired comparison between these two recorded; do not infer statistically significant improvement. Shifted null f=.12: lookahead false correction .39367; correlated null: .418. Under matched model the more elaborate heuristic is not uniformly superior. Results.csv stores paired normal Monte Carlo intervals against rotate only; these are synthetic precision intervals, not human effect sizes. No claim of superiority over SOTA.

## Experiment 2: separate search from certification
Robust gate uses a specified conditional null upper bound u rather than nominal f. If the true root is invalid and conditional success chance q_t <= u given the complete past and chosen action, for any s > u,
L_t/L_(t-1) = s/u for a positive observation, (1-s)/(1-u) otherwise (and 1 on other-root actions).
E[L_t/L_(t-1)|past] = q_t*s/u + (1-q_t)*(1-s)/(1-u) <= 1, since it is increasing in q_t and equals 1 at u. Thus L is a nonnegative test supermartingale starting at 1. Ville + union bound implies probability of ever certifying any invalid root <= alpha using threshold m/alpha, including under adaptive acquisition and optional stopping, conditional on any fixed truth vector for which the bounds hold. No independence across roots required; conditional bound MUST hold for the full history. The claim is false-root certification control, not universal factual accuracy, nor a new mathematical theorem. It does not cover copied results treated as fresh measurements or post hoc bound estimation; bounds must come from design or independent calibration with an allocated error budget.
150,000 additional policy-room trajectories, 5,000 per truth per row. Same 24 paid measurements; search may use nominal or conservative model independently of gate. Matched: nominal .6224 correct recovery, .0012 false correction; gate u=.30 recovery .2772, zero observed false correction. Shift: nominal .5234/.0754; gate u=.12 .4694/.002; conservative search+gate u=.12 .5008/.0018. Persistent hot nulls: nominal .5282/.2844; gate u=.30 .241/.0006; conservative search+gate u=.30 .2486/.0002. u=.12 is not valid for the hot null model even if empirical false corrections happen to be near 5%. Conservative gate loses power. Parameters are constructed, not estimated from real fact-checking. No claim of uniform advantage. 336,000 total V5 trajectories, not independent across paired policies.
