# Research roadmap after V6

Decision 2026-11-26: defer real-model smoke tests, training and human experiments until research resources and supervision are available. No further user-side execution is needed for the proposal milestone. All V1–V6 outputs remain exploratory synthetic or scripted design controls. V5's one-step heuristic did not uniformly outperform posterior greedy; V6's snapshot contrast follows by construction. Do not claim real LLM behavior, SOTA, a proven novel theorem or publication readiness.

## Research question
Can AI-mediated consensus cues and joint access to independent evidence change collective correctability even when the initial claim, underlying source pool, per-person access marginals and budget are matched? Can ordinary selective logs conceal joint failure? Define verified recovery by a designated recipient at a fixed horizon on assigned false claims. Report false correction on true controls, discovery, relay, adoption, burden and coverage separately. Prospective access changes outcomes; nonreactive retrospective readouts measure existing outcomes, not uncreated evidence.

## Planned research packages
1. Updated literature and co-design with ethics approval. Treat classical hidden-profile research (Stasser and Titus 1985, https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.48.6.1467), online search and misinformation (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06883-y), human–AI feedback (https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-02077-2), knowledge collapse (https://www.nber.org/papers/w34910) and distributed LLM reasoning as serious prior work.
2. Formal identification and audit design under policy-dependent joint repair and observability. Compare direct portfolio audit and standard sequential methods; do not treat classical parity constructions, partial identification or controlled sensing as independently new. Use noisy and conflicting fixtures, multiple model families, true-claim controls, cost accounting and independent calibration. Neural graphs and constrained RL are conditional on evidence simpler baselines are inadequate.
3. Human causal study, independently randomised rooms: consensus/provenance cues × joint evidence access. Match starting answers, underlying evidence and query budget; analyse intention-to-treat. Test memory separately; pilot-based power, accessibility, consent, debriefing, no public dissemination of misinformation.
4. Evaluate interventions, including abstention and independent verification. Report null/adverse results and costs; translate findings into design guidance.

## Literature correction
The current abstract of Yan et al. (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03421v2, calls its framework Hi-Agreement, not TIDE. Previous stage notes are historical and may contain the older shorthand. Li, Naito and Shirado (ICML 2026), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/li26ej.html, is also a close competitor. Targeted records were checked; full-text and forward-citation review remains incomplete. Neither external benchmark has been reproduced. The candidate gap is the controlled causal question about human and collective correctability, not generic distributed reasoning.
