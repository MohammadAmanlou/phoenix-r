# PHOENIX-R: adversarial novelty assessment (26 November 2026)

## Decision
**Conditional GO for PhD research, not GO for a top-tier paper yet.** The broad concept of verification collapse, correlated errors, collective fact recovery, diversity of evidence, and AI-induced under-investment in information is already represented in the literature. Neither a new architecture name nor a collection of established modules establishes novelty. The present prototype establishes a controlled synthetic possibility, not the prevalence or causal effect of the mechanism in actual human–AI systems.

## Recommended central claim to test
Policy-induced collective correctability failure under matched individual initial information access: an intervention on the joint source-access distribution can change verified repair and later recipients' correction, while individual access marginals and initial claims remain fixed. A separate randomized post-endpoint readout tests whether ordinary logs conceal this effect. Discovery and observation are different interventions and must not be conflated. The principal estimand is room-level intention-to-treat difference in verified recovery by a fixed horizon, with false corrections on true claims and resource cost as safeguards. A secondary estimand is the difference between log-based estimates and independently audited outcomes. Not a claim that the use of joint assignment, missing-data methods, or a particular neural architecture is novel.

## Critical code/model caveats
- V4 coverage identity and parity/KL constructions are exact only under stated finite models. Current feedback in `dynamic.py` occurs after publication and does not change the first discovery/relay/uptake endpoint. This is not a general endogenous learning simulation.
- `frontier.py` maximizes any qualifying root Q, including false qualification; it is not true verified correction. A future primary objective must count genuinely correct evidence and false corrections separately. Static allocation enumeration is not adaptive policy learning.
- `text_env.py` uses a gold gate dependent on known truth and a deterministic reader. False corrections and grounded recovery from this pilot do not show human or LLM effectiveness. The optional LLM adapter has not been run. No realistic human behavioral model validated.
- Reported percentages are synthetic Monte Carlo outcomes; no significance claim about human behavior. Equal query budget does not guarantee equal token, time, or participant costs.
- V1–V3 lower bounds rely on specific worlds and assumptions. No proof that theorems are globally new, or that robust structural inference outperforms direct audit in real systems.

## Publication gates
1. Reproduce HiddenBench/TIDE, disjoint-evidence routing, adaptive sequential search, and direct randomized portfolio audit with matched costs; compare against static rotation and independent search.
2. Replace gold gate with independent ground-truth evaluation; use held-out tasks and source families; document model versions, token budgets, refusal/invalid output, actual citations and uncertainty; run multiple genuinely different models.
3. Preregister a room-level human experiment with randomized joint assignment and relay, separate lawful post-outcome readout, true-claim control, consent/debrief, pilot-based power and cluster-aware inference. Do not equate synthetic agent behavior with people.
4. Test whether policy coupling changes recovery beyond mere coverage; check when audit provides useful information beyond direct assessment. A null result, reversal, or superiority of simple baselines must be reported.
5. For ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR, require a genuinely distinct problem or algorithmic guarantee with rigorous comparisons; a specialization of known sensing theory plus an idealized simulation is insufficient. CHI/CSCW/ICWSM is the strongest initial fit if causal human evidence is substantial. ACL/EMNLP is plausible if a convincing dynamic verification benchmark and strong real-LLM evidence emerge. Acceptance is not guaranteed.

**Assessment:** broad concept: low distinctiveness; specific combined causal estimand and empirical design: promising but unverified; theory today: limited specialization; algorithm today: preliminary; empirical readiness: low. Do not claim first in the world. The next step is real empirical discrimination rather than adding unmotivated complexity.
